1. Service Description

PostroomIQ is a monday.com app that gives your account intake email addresses. When your team sends or forwards an email to an intake address (for example, a purchase order, invoice, or sales lead), an AI model reads the email and its supported attachments, extracts the fields for that email type, and creates or updates an item on the monday.com board you chose. The original email's text is posted as an update on the item. Items the App is unsure about, and items that match a review rule, go to a review queue for a person on your team. An optional feature that matches extracted values against a lookup board you choose (for example, a customer or vendor list) is planned; it applies only when it is available and you choose a lookup board.

You stay in control. The App writes only to the boards connected to your intake addresses, using the monday.com permissions of the user who set up each address. It never fills payment fields (such as bank details, remit-to, or payment instructions), never sends email on your behalf or to anyone named in an email, and never follows links in an email.

What PostroomIQ is not. Extracted values are produced by an AI model and can be wrong, incomplete, or influenced by the content of the email. PostroomIQ does not guarantee that every value is correct, that every email will be processed, or that every fraudulent or malicious email will be detected. It is a data-entry and review tool: the decision to act on an item (for example, to ship an order or pay an invoice) is yours.

Attachments. The App does not currently attach email attachments to your monday.com items. Each item's update lists the files the email contained. Forward or BCC emails to an intake address from your own mailbox, so the original email and its attachments stay there. Until attachments are stored, do not give an intake address to vendors or customers to email directly: the App keeps no copy of their attachments for you.
