Privacy Policy

Ghost Code — Shopify Theme Audit App

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alpenglow Software LLC ("we," "us," "our"), operator of Ghost Code ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you install and use the App through the Shopify platform.

1. Information We Collect

Data from Shopify APIs

When you install the App, we access the following through the Shopify Admin API using the read_themes scope:

  • Theme files — Liquid templates, sections, snippets, and layout files from your published theme. We access these files in read-only mode to scan for orphaned code left by previously uninstalled apps.
  • Theme metadata — Theme name, ID, and role (whether it is your published theme) to identify which theme was scanned; the theme's name, version and author as listed in the theme's own settings file, and whether it is an Online Store 2.0 theme; and how many unpublished themes your store has, shown in the App's theme health checklist.
  • Store domain: your store's primary domain and myshopify.com domain, used to find your public storefront homepage (see below).

We do not request write access to your themes. We do not modify any theme files.

Optional scopes

You can grant additional read-only scopes from the Permissions card on the App's Settings page, or from the optional link on the App's home page before your first scan. Each one is requested through Shopify's consent screen, and the App does not access that data until you approve it. You can revoke these scopes at any time in your Shopify admin.

  • read_translations: translated content for your store, used to find orphaned translations left by uninstalled apps.
  • read_locales: the languages enabled on your store, used together with read_translations so the App knows which translations to check.
  • read_products: product tags, prices and compare-at prices, and product metafields, used to find orphaned product tags, stale compare-at prices, leftover metafields, and structured-data (JSON-LD) prices in your theme that no longer match your live product prices.
  • read_content: your online store pages, used to find orphaned pages and links to pages that no longer exist.
  • read_online_store_navigation: your URL redirects, and your navigation menus (each menu's name and each menu item's title and link), used to find orphaned redirects left by uninstalled apps and to check your menu labels and links for injected code.

If you do not grant a scope, the App skips the checks that depend on it.

Your public storefront homepage

When the App scans your published theme, our server loads your store's public homepage once per scan (again only if that step is retried after an error), the same page any visitor sees. If your homepage redirects, the App follows up to 3 redirects, and only to your primary domain or your myshopify.com domain. If it redirects anywhere else, the App skips this check. We use the page only for four things: to find the list of script addresses that Shopify adds for your installed apps ("script tags"); to find "missing snippet" Liquid error messages that Shopify has printed on the page (for example "Liquid error (layout/theme line 309): Could not find asset snippets/example.liquid"), which we report only when your theme files confirm the snippet is missing; to check whether the page loads code from a domain on our list of known malicious domains (in its script and link tags, the scripts Shopify adds for your apps, web addresses written inside the page's scripts, and web addresses in JavaScript attached to page elements, such as an image's onerror attribute); and to confirm it is a Shopify page that is not password-protected. We do not use or keep any other text on the page. Our requests identify themselves with the User-Agent GhostCode/1.0 (+https://alpenglowsoftware.com). We do not log in, submit forms, or send cookies. We do not collect information about your visitors or customers. If your storefront is password-protected, the App does not try to get past the password page and skips this check. Before a store speed report, the App also loads your homepage once in the same way, only to check whether it is password-protected.

Checkout guard: scheduled checks of your public storefront

The App also checks your live storefront on a schedule: once an hour on the Standard and Professional plans, and once a day on the Free plan. Each check loads your public homepage and then your cart page (/cart, with nothing in the cart), the same pages any visitor sees, in the same way as the homepage read above: the same User-Agent, at most 3 redirects and only to your primary domain or your myshopify.com domain, no logging in, no form submissions, and no cookies. If your storefront is password-protected or redirects to another domain, the check pauses and tries again a few hours later. To know which addresses to load, we read your store's primary domain and myshopify.com domain from the Shopify Admin API at most once a day and keep a copy. We use the pages only to find the code they load or run and the web addresses (hosts) that code comes from, so we can warn you about code from a domain on our list of known malicious domains, code that loads other scripts in an unusual way (for example inside your menu or checkout button), and script sources that are new to your store. We do not keep the pages' text, and we do not collect information about your visitors or customers.

Data collected through Shopify session

Shopify provides the following data as part of the standard app authentication process:

  • Shop domain
  • Staff member name, email address, and user ID
  • Account role (store owner or collaborator) and email verification status
  • Locale preference
  • Session tokens, access tokens, and refresh tokens

This session data is managed by Shopify's official session storage library and is required for the App to function within the Shopify Admin.

Store owner email address and store name

If your store receives the summary email (see Section 2), we read your store owner's email address and your store's name from the Shopify Admin API. Shopify describes the email as the address it uses to communicate with the shop owner. Reading them does not require any additional scope. It is not your public customer-facing contact email, and it is not a staff member's email address.

We store a copy of both on your shop record and refresh them from Shopify from time to time while the App is installed. We use the email address only to send the summary email and checkout guard alert emails (see Section 2) and to show you, in the App, which address they go to. We use the store name only to address these emails to your store.

Information you provide

  • Help messages and feedback: if you send a message from the in-app Help page, we collect the topic you choose (for example, a problem, a how-to question, feedback, or billing), your message, and, for feedback only, an optional satisfaction rating (1 to 5). You may also give a contact email address. We store it only if you also tick the box allowing us to email you about that request, and we record when you sent the message with that box ticked. Messages sent through the earlier feedback form may include a contact email you left there for follow-up, without that box, because the earlier form did not have one. When you report a problem, we automatically attach your plan and the ID of your latest scan so you do not have to look them up. Those earlier messages also included answers to up to three optional free-text questions.
  • How you found the App: once your scan results are showing, the App asks how you found it, until you answer or skip the question. If you answer, we store your choice from a fixed list (for example, App Store search, Shopify Sidekick, an AI assistant, or a search engine) and, if you choose "Other", the short text you type. You can skip the question.
  • App name suggestions: if you suggest which app an unrecognized script belongs to, we store your suggestion for review.
  • Ignored findings: if you choose to ignore a finding, we store that choice and any optional note you add.
  • Email preference: whether the summary email is turned on or off for your store, and when we told you about it in the App or you turned it on yourself.
  • Checkout guard email consent: when the App showed you the notice that checkout guard alerts will be emailed (or you turned them on in Settings), when you turned them off, and a fingerprint (hash) of the email address the notice named, so alerts go only to that address. We do not store the address itself for this purpose.

Usage of the App

The App records your use of its own pages so we can operate and improve it. These are server-side records kept in our own database:

  • A page visit record (your shop domain and the App page you opened) each time an App page is loaded.
  • The last time your store opened the App.
  • Whether in-app prompts (for example, the feedback prompt, the "how did you find us" question, or an upgrade preview) were shown to you, clicked, dismissed, skipped, or completed.
  • Technical error reports when an App page fails in your browser: the error message, a short code location trace, the App page path (without any query string), the HTTP status of a failed request, and your browser family (for example, Chrome). We strip query strings, tokens, and email addresses from these reports before storing them, and delete them after 30 days.
  • Server error records when a request to the App fails on our server, including a request from Shopify Sidekick: an error code, the time, and your shop domain, and for some errors the App page path or a short error description. They never contain your theme's code or information about your visitors or customers.

This does not use cookies or third-party analytics tools, and it does not record anything outside the App's own pages.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Customer or shopper personal information
  • Order or transaction data
  • Payment or financial information
  • Storefront visitor analytics or behavior data
  • Marketing or advertising data
  • Any content behind your storefront password

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect solely to provide and improve the App's services:

  • Theme scanning — We analyze your theme files in memory to detect orphaned code patterns (ghost scripts, stylesheets, snippets, and sections) left by previously uninstalled apps.
  • Scan results — We store findings (file name, line number, a short code snippet of up to 300 characters, severity, and the identified app name) so you can review results in the App.
  • Optional-scope findings: for checks that use an optional scope, findings store short excerpts (up to 300 characters) and identifiers such as tag names, product or page handles, metafield keys with short value excerpts, and redirect paths. When a menu item's label or link contains code (for example a script, or a link to a domain on our list of known malicious domains), we store a finding with the menu and menu item IDs, the item's place in the menu (the menu's name and the titles of the item and the items above it, each cut to 80 characters), an excerpt of up to 200 characters of the label or link around the code, and a fingerprint (hash) of the full label and link so we can tell when the item changes. We use it only to warn you in the App and in the Sidekick scan summary. Full product, page, translation, menu, and redirect data is not stored.
  • Third-party script domains: for each scan we store the third-party domains your theme references and any unrecognized script URLs, with the file name, line number, and a short code snippet. We may analyze this data in aggregate across stores to improve how the App recognizes apps. This data is never sold, and it is deleted with the rest of your shop data as described in Section 6.
  • Storefront script tags: for each scan we store the web addresses of the scripts that Shopify loads on your storefront for your apps, with query strings and fragments removed, kept in a short excerpt of up to 300 characters per app, along with the app we identify for each one. These addresses belong to third-party apps and may contain identifiers those apps use for your store. Your store's file ID in addresses on Shopify's CDN (cdn.shopify.com) is removed. We use them to warn you about apps that will stop working when Shopify stops running script tags on March 1, 2027. This data is deleted with the rest of your shop data as described in Section 6.
  • Known malicious code on your live store: if your homepage uses a domain on our list of known malicious domains, we store a finding with that domain, the web address host as it appeared on the page (no path or query string), and how the page used it (for example, loaded by a script tag an app added). If a store speed test's browser requests an address on such a domain, we store that domain with the test's summary. We use these only to warn you in the App. Homepage findings are part of your scan results, so they also count toward the summary email's finding counts and can be listed in the Sidekick scan summary (see below). This data is deleted with the rest of your shop data as described in Section 6.
  • Scan diagnostics: for each scan we keep an internal record of how the scan ran: timings, file counts and sizes, how many findings each check produced and, for some checks we are still testing, the theme file names and line numbers where a pattern was found, with your theme's name. It contains no theme code, no menu content, and no personal data. We use it only to measure and improve the App's checks. It is deleted with the rest of your shop data as described in Section 6.
  • Help messages and feedback: we use your messages to answer you, fix problems, and improve the App. We email you about a message only if you gave an email address and agreed to be contacted about it.
  • How you found the App: we use your answer, combined with other merchants' answers, to understand which channels bring merchants to the App and where to spend our marketing effort.
  • Usage records: we use records of your use of the App's pages and prompts to operate and improve the App.
  • App removal records: Shopify does not tell us which apps you install or remove. When an app was active in your store at one scan (its app embed was switched on in your theme, or it loaded through a storefront script tag) and is no longer active at the next scan of the same theme, while its code is still in your theme, we infer that the app is no longer active and record what it left behind. For each removal we detect, we store your shop ID, the theme ID, the app's name, the IDs of the scan that detected it and of the previous scan, the number of leftover items, its status (removed, cleaned up, or reinstalled), and when it was detected and last updated. Later scans update the status. These records contain no code and no personal data. We use them to show these apps in the App and in the summary email. Because this is an inference, it can miss a removal (for example, an app that left no code behind, or an app we cannot see as active) or, rarely, report one that did not happen (for example, if you only switched an app's embed off).
  • Active apps at scan time: each scan stores the names of the apps it saw as active in your store (apps whose app embed was switched on, and apps loading through storefront script tags). We use this only to detect apps that are no longer active, as described above.
  • App signature fingerprints: each scan stores a fingerprint (hash) of each of the App's built-in app signatures as they were when the scan ran. These describe our own detection rules, not your store. We use them so that an update to our rules is never mistaken for an app removal.
  • Summary email: after each scheduled scan, we email your store owner's email address a summary of what changed in your scans. On the Professional plan this is weekly; on the Standard plan it is monthly. On the Standard and Professional plans, a noticeable change in your store speed report can also be a reason to send it. Free plan stores do not receive it. The email contains your store name (or your myshopify domain if no name is available), the apps we detected as no longer active since the previous summary (or, for your first summary, since the previous scan), each with the number of items it left behind or a note that they were cleaned up, the number of new findings, fixed findings, and findings still in your theme, on the Standard and Professional plans the result of your latest store speed report (your homepage speed score and the app that used the most processing time, and on the Professional plan up to three apps that are new or used noticeably more processing time or data than in the test before) when it changed noticeably or is your first report, a link to the App in your Shopify admin, an unsubscribe link, and our company name, logo, and contact email. The logo image is loaded from our website when you open the email. We do not use open tracking (tracking pixels) or click tracking in the summary email: its links are not rewritten and we are not told when you open it or click a link. It does not contain code snippets, theme file names, or any customer data. We send at most one summary email per store for each scheduled scan. If nothing changed since the previous summary (no new or fixed findings, no app newly inactive or cleaned up, and on the Standard and Professional plans no noticeable change in your store speed report), we do not send one.
  • Summary email records: for each summary email we send, we store your shop ID, the ID of the scan it summarized, the recipient email address, the time it was sent, the counts it reported, and a fingerprint (hash) of what it reported. We use these records to avoid sending duplicate emails and to confirm what was sent. We also store a random unsubscribe code on your shop record so the unsubscribe link in each email works.
  • Checkout guard: for your store we keep your primary and myshopify.com domains, the time and result of the latest check (for example "password page" or "could not be read"), retry timing, the script hosts the guard has learned as normal for your store, and the hosts seen in its last few checks. For each thing it reports we keep its type, the host (no path or query string), the page it was seen on (homepage or cart page), a fixed description of where it was found (for example "Loaded by a script tag"), when it was first and last seen, its status, and whether you marked it as expected. We use this only to show these alerts in the App on every plan, to list known malicious code and unusual script loaders in the Sidekick scan summary, and to send checkout guard alert emails. This data is deleted as described in Section 6.
  • Checkout guard alert email: on the Standard and Professional plans, when the guard finds code from a known malicious domain or a confirmed unusual script loader, we email your store owner's email address. We send it only after the App has shown you a notice naming that address (or you turned these alerts on in Settings), only to the address the notice named, and not after you turn the alerts off or unsubscribe from GhostCode emails. We send at most one email per incident and at most 3 per store per day; free plan stores are never emailed. The email contains your store name (or your myshopify domain), for each incident (up to 10) a fixed description naming the host and the page it was seen on, steps to check, a link to the App in your Shopify admin, a link to a checklist on our website, an unsubscribe link, and our company name and contact email. It does not contain page text, code from your store, or any customer data. As with the summary email, we do not use open or click tracking. For each alert email we store your shop ID, a reference to the incidents it reported, how many it reported, the recipient email address, and the time it was sent, and one record per reported incident with the recipient address so an incident is never emailed twice.
  • Store speed report: on the Professional plan, once a week and when you click Run now (at most once a day), we ask Google PageSpeed Insights to test your storefront homepage and one product page. On the Standard plan, we run the same test once a month. On the Free plan, when you click Run your free speed test, we ask Google PageSpeed Insights to test your storefront homepage only, once. If that test can't finish, you can try again, at most once a day, and we may stop offering it after several tests that could not be completed. We limit how many free tests run each day, so a free test can be paused until the next day. For this test we do not read your products or your public product list. To choose the product page, we use the Shopify Admin API if you have granted Products access; otherwise we read your store's public product list (/products.json on your primary domain, the same public information any visitor can see) and pick one published product. We keep only the chosen page's address. We send Google only the public addresses (URLs) of those pages; Google's servers then load the pages the way a visitor would. Because of that, the test can appear as a visit in your store's analytics. We store a summary of each test: the speed scores and timings, page weight and request counts, how much time and data each app or third-party service on the page used, and any domains on our list of known malicious domains that the test requested. We do not store the pages' content. If your storefront is password protected, the test cannot run and we record only that.
  • Shopify Sidekick (optional): if you ask Shopify Sidekick, Shopify's AI assistant in your Shopify Admin, about your theme scan or your store speed, Sidekick asks GhostCode for a summary and GhostCode returns it. The scan summary contains the date and theme name of your latest successful scan, how many findings it has in total and in each impact area (for example "Speed"), the findings GhostCode suggests starting with (each with its type, impact area, app name if known, theme file and line, or "Menu item" for code found in a menu, and a one-line description of what it costs you), any known malicious code with where it was found (its theme file and line, or your live homepage), app name if known and our description, and your theme health summary (which checks came back clean or did not fully run, your theme's name, version and type, and how many themes your store has), how many app security advisories your latest scan found, open checkout guard alerts for known malicious code or unusual script loaders on your live store (up to 5, each with a fixed description, the page it was seen on, and when it was first and last seen; also when no scan has completed yet), and the names of the unpublished themes you have scanned with how many findings each had in its last scan. On the Free plan, the findings listed are only those the App already shows you in full; known malicious code is listed on every plan. The speed summary contains the date of your latest speed result, the pages tested, their speed score and timings, and the apps that used the most processing time and data. On the Standard and Professional plans that is up to five apps per tested page; on the Free plan it is your free homepage test and the one app that used the most processing time. It never contains your theme's code, information about your visitors or customers, or access tokens. GhostCode does not receive your questions or Sidekick's replies, and it does not store the summaries it returns. The tools are read-only: Sidekick cannot use them to change your theme, run a scan or start a speed test. Sidekick is operated by Shopify, which processes your conversations, including these summaries, under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Subscription management — We store your current billing plan to gate features appropriately.
  • App functionality — We use your shop domain and session data to authenticate requests and deliver the App within the Shopify Admin.

3. How We Process Theme and Storefront Data

Your theme files are processed as follows:

  1. Theme files are fetched from the Shopify API and processed in memory.
  2. Pattern matching detects orphaned code artifacts.
  3. Only the scan findings (metadata and short code snippets) are stored in our database.
  4. Data accessed through optional scopes is processed the same way: only findings are stored.
  5. Full theme file contents are not stored — they are discarded after processing.
  6. Your public storefront homepage is fetched directly from your store over HTTPS and processed in memory. Only three things are kept: the list of script addresses (without query strings or fragments); for each confirmed Liquid error, the theme file path, line number and snippet name from the error message; and, for each known malicious domain the page uses, that domain, the matched host (no path or query string) and how the page used it. The page itself is not stored.
  7. The homepage and cart page read by each checkout guard check are processed in memory the same way. Only host names, fixed descriptions, and timestamps are kept, as described in Section 2. The pages themselves are not stored.
  8. The list of web addresses requested during each store speed test, which comes back from Google PageSpeed Insights with the test result, is checked in memory against our list of known malicious domains. Only the matched domains are kept, with the test's summary.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway (United States).
  • Our hosting provider encrypts all stored data at rest at the storage level, including Shopify access tokens. Access tokens are used only by our servers to call the Shopify API and are deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not use your store owner's email address to send marketing emails.

6. Data Retention

  • While installed — Scan results, findings, and shop data are retained for as long as the App is installed on your store.
  • Page visit records: deleted after 14 days.
  • In-app prompt interaction records: deleted after 90 days.
  • Technical error reports: deleted after 30 days.
  • Server error records: kept until your shop data is deleted.
  • Scan diagnostics records: kept until your shop data is deleted.
  • Help messages, feedback, and how you found the App: kept until your shop data is deleted.
  • App removal records: kept until your shop data is deleted. A record is not deleted if the app is reinstalled; its status changes instead.
  • Store speed reports, including the free speed test and when it was used: kept until your shop data is deleted.
  • Summary email records and your stored store owner email address and store name: kept until your shop data is deleted.
  • Checkout guard data: a resolved alert is deleted 30 days after it was resolved; a learned script host the guard has not seen for 30 days is dropped. Records of each checkout guard email are kept until your shop data is deleted, except the per-incident records, which are deleted 90 days after the email was sent. The rest is kept until your shop data is deleted.
  • On uninstall: your sessions and Shopify access tokens are deleted immediately, and your shop is marked as uninstalled. The rest of your data is kept until Shopify sends a shop data erasure (shop/redact) request, which Shopify sends 48 hours after you uninstall. If you reinstall before then, your existing data is still available.
  • Shop data erasure: when we receive the shop/redact request, we permanently delete all data associated with your shop, including scans, findings, script domain records, scan diagnostics records, help messages and feedback, your answer about how you found the App, usage records, app removal records, store speed reports, checkout guard data, summary and checkout guard email records, and your shop record (including the stored store owner email address, store name, and unsubscribe code).
  • Email copies: help message notifications, our email replies to you, and operational alerts already delivered to our inboxes are not part of the App's database and are not removed by this automated deletion. Contact us to have them deleted.
  • Emails you received: summary and checkout guard emails already delivered to your store owner's inbox are outside our control and are not removed by this automated deletion.
  • Aggregate statistics: summary counts that do not identify your store (for example, total scans across all stores) may be kept after your data is deleted.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — View all scan data and findings through the App's dashboard.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the App deletes your sessions immediately and all remaining shop data when Shopify sends the shop/redact request 48 hours later (see Section 6). You can also contact us to request deletion without uninstalling.
  • Data portability — Contact us to request an export of your stored data.
  • Correction — Contact us if you believe any stored data is inaccurate.
  • Opt out of the summary email: the email is on by default for stores on the Standard and Professional plans, but we send it only after we have told you in the App that it is on, or after you turn it on yourself. Turn it off at any time in the App's Settings page, or use the one-click unsubscribe link in any summary email. Unsubscribing stops this email and checkout guard alert emails. It does not change your plan or your scans.
  • Opt out of checkout guard alert emails: turn them off from the notice on the App's home page or in the App's Settings page, or use the unsubscribe link in any GhostCode email. Turning the summary email back on does not turn checkout guard alert emails back on. Alerts are still shown in the App.

We respond to all data requests within 30 days.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data is processed and stored on servers located in the United States. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your data to the United States for processing.

9. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party analytics or advertising technologies. The App records your use of its own pages on our servers to operate and improve it, as described in Section 1. It does not track your activity elsewhere in the Shopify Admin.

The App is designed for use by Shopify merchants (business users) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
