1. Service Description

IntakePilot is a Shopify app that turns wholesale purchase orders (POs) into Shopify draft orders. You forward a PO email to your store's forwarding address, or upload a PO file or paste an order's text in the App. An AI model reads the document, the App matches the buyer and the items to your Shopify companies, customers and products, and it creates a draft order (or updates or flags an existing one when a PO changes or cancels an earlier order). The App shows each PO next to its draft for review, and a member of your staff approves it before it becomes an order.

A person approves every order. The App never turns a draft into an order on its own: a draft becomes an order only when a member of your staff approves it in the App (which refuses approval while anything on the PO is unresolved) or completes the draft themselves in Shopify. Apart from creating and updating draft orders, adding its own tags to them, completing a draft you approve, and creating a customer when you press "Add customer in Shopify", the App does not change your store. It does not edit orders after they are placed (a change that arrives after an order exists is flagged for you instead), it does not change products or prices, and it never contacts your buyers: it sends them no email, invoice or other message.

What IntakePilot is not. IntakePilot is not an order-entry service that guarantees accuracy, a pricing or contract tool, or a substitute for checking a PO against your agreement with the buyer. It is an assistant that reads POs and prepares drafts; the decision to accept an order, and the order itself, remain yours.
