Privacy Policy

DedupeIQ: Merge Duplicates for monday.com

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alpenglow Software LLC ("we," "us," "our"), operator of DedupeIQ ("the App"), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you install and use the App through the monday.com platform. DedupeIQ finds duplicate and near-duplicate items on your monday.com boards, lets you review each match, and, only when you choose to, merges a confirmed pair by filling in the item you keep and archiving the other.

The short version: matching runs in your browser. The item names and column values on your boards (names, emails, phone numbers, companies, and any other column) are read and compared on your device and are never sent to or stored on our servers. Our servers store only IDs, counts, match scores, and your review decisions.

1. Information We Collect

Permissions (monday.com OAuth scopes)

  • boards:read: used to read the columns and items of the boards you choose to scan, so duplicates can be detected. This reading happens in your browser.
  • boards:write: used only when you explicitly merge a confirmed pair (or keep one of two records across boards). The App writes the values you chose to the item you keep and archives the other item. Scanning and reviewing never write to your boards.
  • me:read: used to identify the signed-in monday.com user so decisions and merges can be attributed to a user ID.

Data processed in your browser only (never sent to us)

  • Item names and column values of the boards you scan, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, company names, and the other columns shown when you compare or merge two items.
  • Match explanations (for example, "nickname: Bob and Robert"). Before anything leaves your browser, these are converted to value-free codes (for example, "name nickname match"), which contain no names, emails, or phone numbers.

Data we store (on monday.com's infrastructure)

  • Scan summaries: your monday.com account ID, the board ID(s) scanned, the user ID that ran the scan, the number of items read and found, match counts, and which column IDs were used for name, email, phone, and company.
  • Review decisions: for each pair you confirm or dismiss, the two item IDs, the decision, the match band and confidence score, the user ID, and the time. Undoing a decision deletes it.
  • Merge records: for each merge you perform, the item IDs involved, the IDs of columns filled or chosen, the user ID, and whether the archive step succeeded. No column values are recorded.
  • Usage events: product events such as "scan run" or "upgrade prompt shown," with IDs and counts only.
  • Plan and subscription status: your current DedupeIQ plan, trial status, and related dates, as reported by monday.com's billing system, plus counters used to apply plan limits (for example, scans this month or merges used in a trial).
  • Rate-limit counters: a per-account daily count of database writes, used to protect the service from abuse.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Item names or column values from your boards (no names, emails, phone numbers, companies, notes, files, or updates)
  • monday.com user names or email addresses
  • Payment card or banking information (billing is handled entirely by monday.com)
  • Tracking, advertising, or web-analytics data

We do not use artificial intelligence or machine learning to process your data, and your data is not used to train AI or machine-learning models. Matching is performed by deterministic statistical comparison running in your browser.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Resume and remember: scan summaries and decisions let you reopen a board where you left off, and keep dismissed pairs dismissed when you scan again.
  • Merging at your direction: merge records provide an audit trail of merges you performed.
  • Plans and limits: plan status and counters let us apply the features and limits of your plan.
  • Improving the App: aggregate usage events (counts, not content) help us understand which features are used.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising, marketing, or profiling. DedupeIQ never merges or changes anything automatically: every merge is started by a member of your team.

For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, you are the data controller for your board data. Alpenglow Software LLC acts as a data processor for the limited identifiers described above, processing them solely on your behalf and as described in this policy.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • The App runs entirely on monday.com infrastructure: the interface is served from monday.com's app hosting, and our server and database run on monday code, monday.com's hosting platform, in the United States region.
  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.
  • Every request to our server is authenticated with a monday.com-signed session token, and all stored data is scoped to your monday.com account.
  • In the event of a data breach affecting your data, we will notify you and monday.com promptly after discovery, in accordance with monday.com's marketplace requirements and applicable law.

5. Data Retention

  • While installed: scan summaries, decisions, merge records, and events are kept while the App is installed, so your review history persists.
  • On uninstall: when you uninstall the App, monday.com notifies us and we immediately and permanently delete all data associated with your account (scan summaries, decisions, merge records, events, and plan status). The only exception is the rate-limit counter for the current day, which contains your account ID, the date, and a number, and is deleted automatically after that day. It is kept so that uninstalling cannot be used to bypass abuse limits.
  • Your boards: uninstalling does not change your boards. Items you merged stay merged, and items the App archived remain in your board's archive under monday.com's policies, where you can restore them.

6. Your Rights

  • Access and portability: contact us to request a copy of the data we store for your account in a machine-readable format.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the App deletes your data (see Section 5). You may also contact us to request deletion without uninstalling.
  • Correction: you can change any review decision in the App at any time.

Because we do not store the contents of your boards, requests about a specific person's data (for example, a contact on your CRM board) are fulfilled in monday.com itself. We store no names, emails, or phone numbers that could be searched. We respond to all data requests within 30 days.

7. International Data Transfers

Our server and database run in monday.com's United States region. By using the App, you consent to the processing of the identifiers described above in the United States.

8. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or advertising technologies. Usage events are recorded on our own server as described above and contain no board content.

9. Children's Privacy
