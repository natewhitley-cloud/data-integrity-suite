1. Service Description

FraudPilot is a Shopify app that gives you a human-review queue for orders that Shopify's risk engine flags as medium or high risk. The App enriches each flagged order with the order facts, Shopify's own risk signals, geographic and network context, order velocity, and your own prior decisions for the same buyer, so you can review a risky order in one place. You then record your decision (approve, cancel, or tag), and the App keeps an immutable, append-only decision log that you can export as chargeback-representment evidence.

The App operates in review, enrichment, and record-keeping mode only. FraudPilot is read-only against your Shopify data. It does not cancel, hold, modify, refund, fulfill, or tag orders on your behalf; it does not block or decline any order or customer; and it does not make any automated decision about an order. Recording a decision in FraudPilot does not carry that action out in Shopify. Every action on an order is completed by you in the Shopify Admin, and every decision recorded in the App is one you made.

What FraudPilot is not. FraudPilot is not a fraud-prevention guarantee, fraud insurance, or a chargeback-protection service. It does not guarantee that you will win any chargeback or dispute. It is not an automatic order canceller, an order-blocking tool, or a country/IP blocker. It is a decision-support and record-keeping tool that equips you to decide and to document your decision; the judgment, the action, and the outcome remain yours.
